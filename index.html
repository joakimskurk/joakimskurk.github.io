joakimskurk

Apps, games and instruments for the M-VAVE FM-1, built on Dinghy. None of them has been tried on a real FM-1 yet, so install at your own risk, and tell us how it went.

VaveHero

A rhythm game: notes slide down a highway, you play them on the white keys while the key LEDs show what is coming. Two players with their own FM-1 can play against each other.

Open Source

Star Distroyer

A starship FM synth: four banks of oscillators on an X/Y nav field, a morphing resonator, multiband destruction, delay and room.

Open Source

FM-1 Clock

A pendulum wall clock whose gong is rebuilt as an FM synth of some four hundred operators, and a carved cuckoo clock whose bird calls the hours.

Open Source

RoboSeq

A sequencer that is a board game: two robots walk a 5×5 board of conveyor belts, zap rails and rotators, and where they go is the music. Steer them yourself, or battle a friend on the beat.

Source

SkurkyMello

A generative step sequencer and synth: a sixteen-step melody at its heart, with a bass, chords, a four-voice drum kit and a sample layer around it.
